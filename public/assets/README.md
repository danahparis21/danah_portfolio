# 📁 Assets Organization Guide for Danah's Portfolio

Welcome! You can drop your videos, images, certificates, and resume into these dedicated folders.

---

### 1. 🎥 Videos (`public/assets/videos/`)
* **`projects/`**: Video walkthroughs or screen recordings of your projects.
  * *Recommended format*: `.mp4` (H.264 codec) or `.webm`
  * *Recommended resolution*: 1080p (1920x1080) or 720p (1280x720)
  * *Tip*: Keep file sizes under 15-25MB each for quick streaming without lag on mobile/desktop.
* **`achievements/`**:
  * `self-pitch.mp4` -> Your Technopreneurship self-pitch introduction video!
  * `best-advocacy.mp4` -> Your award-winning advocacy video!

---

### 2. 🖼️ Images (`public/assets/images/`)
* **`avatar/`**:
  * Your headshot, portrait, or 3D avatar (transparent PNG or high-res JPG) for the interactive 3D ID badge card.
* **`projects/`**:
  * Thumbnail posters / cover shots for your projects (shown before video plays).
* **`certificates/`**:
  * Scans or PNG exports of your awards, course certificates, and hackathon recognitions.
* **`stickers/`**:
  * Fun macOS / iOS stickers, emojis, icons, or badges.
* **`bakery/`**:
  * Custom cupcake bases, frostings, toppings, pastry SVGs/PNGs (or we can render them procedurally with SVG/Canvas!).

---

### 3. 📄 Documents (`public/assets/documents/`)
* **`resume/`**:
  * Drop your latest resume here (e.g. `Danah_Paris_Resume.pdf`). We will link a "Download Resume" button in the macOS dock & header.

---

### 4. 🔊 Sounds (`public/assets/sounds/` - Optional)
* Subtle Apple-like UI clicks, pop sounds, window minimize sound effects (`.mp3` or `.wav`).
